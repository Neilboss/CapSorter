Introducing CapSorter: a 100% offline, dual-controller bottle-cap sorting robot built in two open-source stacks (Teensy 4.1 + ESP32-CAM and Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W + 160° IMX219 + 3.5" SPI LCD) over a shared Arduino Mega 2560 motion and fail-safe layer. Pipelined bin-side re-imaging adapts to bumped caps mid-cycle (100% completion vs. 67.5% open-loop), complete with Stages 0–8 physical bring-up scripts, a 15-page paper, and a live 2D simulator at capsorter.vercel.app (github.com/Neilboss/CapSorter).
