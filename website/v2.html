Compact self-contained tabletop footprint constructed with 2020 aluminum extrusions. The 160° wide-angle lens allows flexible top camera mounting from 220–450 mm height.

Standard Bottle Caps

Engineered to handle common plastic beverage bottle caps with outer diameter between Ø28 mm and Ø32 mm across 4 color classes.

300×300 mm Grid Workspace

10 × 10 sorting grid (30 mm pitch) with matte neutral background and 4 corner fiducial markers for OpenCV homography calibration.

Single-Board Capture & Vision

Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W directly captures frames from the 8MP IMX219 CSI camera (or USB webcam), undistorts fisheye curvature, segments HSV masks, and computes mm coordinates.

Cartesian XY + Z Gripper

Two NEMA 17 stepper motors on GT2 belts move the gantry; two MG90S metal-gear servos control vertical stroke and parallel gripper jaws.

Designated Color Bins

Four color-coded bins (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) positioned outside the active grid area for unobstructed sorting.

16 GB Air-Gapped Edge OS

Boots headless Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) with Wi-Fi disabled. Auto-starts the Python OpenCV sorting service via systemd with zero internet dependency.

Fail-Safe Comms Recovery

If UART communication between Pi Zero 2 W and Mega drops mid-pick, the Mega autonomously finishes depositing the held cap, retracts Z, and parks safely.

Why Keep the Arduino Mega 2560 Alongside the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W? The Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W runs a multitasking Linux kernel where background OS interrupts can introduce 50–500 µs timing jitter on GPIO pins—which causes stepper motors to stutter or miss steps at high speeds. By keeping the Arduino Mega 2560 as a dedicated real-time motion coprocessor, stepper acceleration ramps remain deterministic while a hardwired dual-pole E-stop cuts actuator power independently of software.

Pi Zero 2 W & Mega Logic

Separate regulated 5V logic rail (common star GND — not galvanically isolated) powering Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, Arduino Mega 5V pin, and PCA9685 VCC. Combined 5V/5A + 5V/3A = 40W rated (~45–47W at 12V input); verify buck efficiency, wire gauges, fuses & connectors.

  • • Pi Zero 2 W 5V (Pins 2 & 4 / USB PWR)
  • • 8MP IMX219 powered via Pi CSI port
  • • Arduino Mega 2560 5V pin (NOT VIN; avoid USB backfeed) & PCA9685 VCC
MANDATORY 3.3V ⇄ 5V LEVEL SHIFTER WARNING (PI ZERO 2 W GPIO): Just like the Teensy 4.1 in V1, the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W GPIO pins operate strictly at 3.3V logic and are NOT 5V tolerant. The Arduino Mega 2560 operates at 5V logic. Connecting Arduino Mega Pin 18 (TX1 5V) directly to Pi Zero 2 W Pin 10 (GPIO 15 / RXD0) will permanently damage the Pi SoC. Always route UART through the 4-channel bidirectional logic level shifter (HV=5V, LV=3.3V).

Comms-Loss Finish & Park Halt

On UART heartbeat loss (>1.2 s), if a scanned cap is already mid-cycle, the Mega finishes placing only that one active cap into its assigned color bin (skipping any further scans/caps), returns cleanly to (0, 0) (STATE_FAILSAFE_PARKING), disables steppers, and latches STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT until explicit operator re-arm (CMD_REARM_HOME) & re-homing.

NC Limit Switches & Homing

Three normally closed (NC) mechanical limit switches monitor X, Y, and Z (Pins 22–24, polled in loop() with 3-sample debounce — Port A has no AVR ISR). Open-circuit or overtravel trips latch STATE_HARD_FAULT and disable STEPPER_EN and SERVO_OE. Measure actual stop time & distance on the machine.

Hardwired Dual-Pole E-Stop

Pole 1 of a latching red mushroom E-Stop cuts 12V actuator power to TMC2208 VMOT and the Branch B 5V servo buck input independently of firmware while keeping the Pi Zero 2 W powered on Branch C (protecting the SD card). Pole 2 signals Mega Pin 25 so reset never causes unexpected motion; use a mechanical Z counterbalance/stop for torque loss on power cut.

class CalibrationError(RuntimeError):

def crc16_ccitt(data: bytes) -> int:
    crc = 0xFFFF
    for b in data:
        crc ^= (b << 8)
        for _ in range(8):
            crc = ((crc << 1) ^ 0x1021) & 0xFFFF if (crc & 0x8000) else (crc << 1) & 0xFFFF
    return crc


def load_and_validate_calibration(
    calib_file: str,
    expected_camera_type: str,
    expected_size: tuple,
) -> dict:
    """
    Fail-closed calibration loader. Refuses missing files, uncalibrated placeholders,
    mismatched camera types (e.g. CSI calibration on USB webcam), resolution mismatches,
    or malformed/non-finite matrices.
    """
    if not calib_file or not os.path.isfile(calib_file):
        raise CalibrationError(
            f"Calibration file not found: '{calib_file}'. "
            f"Run fisheye_calibrate.py for camera_type='{expected_camera_type}' before enabling motion."
        )

    try:
        cal = np.load(calib_file, allow_pickle=False)
    except Exception as exc:
        raise CalibrationError(f"Failed to read calibration archive '{calib_file}': {exc}") from exc

    required = ("K", "D", "H", "camera_type", "lens_model", "image_size", "calibrated")
    missing = [k for k in required if k not in cal.files]
    if missing:
        raise CalibrationError(
            f"Calibration file '{calib_file}' is missing required keys {missing}. "
            "Re-generate with fisheye_calibrate.py."
        )

    is_calibrated = bool(np.asarray(cal["calibrated"]).item())
    if not is_calibrated:
        raise CalibrationError(
            f"Calibration file '{calib_file}' is marked uncalibrated (calibrated=False). "
            "Motion inhibited until real checkerboard + fiducial calibration is completed."
        )

    cal_camera_type = str(np.asarray(cal["camera_type"]).item())
    if cal_camera_type != expected_camera_type:
        raise CalibrationError(
            f"Calibration camera_type mismatch: file has '{cal_camera_type}', "
            f"but active camera is '{expected_camera_type}'. Use a calibration file "
            "captured on this exact camera and lens."
        )

    lens_model = str(np.asarray(cal["lens_model"]).item())
    if lens_model not in ("fisheye", "pinhole"):
        raise CalibrationError(f"Unsupported lens_model '{lens_model}' in '{calib_file}'.")

    cal_size = tuple(int(v) for v in np.asarray(cal["image_size"]).reshape(-1))
    if cal_size != tuple(expected_size):
        raise CalibrationError(
            f"Calibration image_size {cal_size} does not match configured capture size {tuple(expected_size)}."
        )

    try:
        K = np.asarray(cal["K"], dtype=np.float64).reshape((3, 3))
        H = np.asarray(cal["H"], dtype=np.float64).reshape((3, 3))
        D = np.asarray(cal["D"], dtype=np.float64).reshape((-1, 1))
    except ValueError as exc:
        raise CalibrationError(f"Invalid matrix dimensions in '{calib_file}': {exc}") from exc

    if lens_model == "fisheye" and D.shape != (4, 1):
        raise CalibrationError(f"Fisheye distortion vector D must have shape (4, 1), got {D.shape}.")
    if lens_model == "pinhole" and D.shape[0] < 4:
        raise CalibrationError(f"Pinhole distortion vector D must have >=4 coefficients, got {D.shape}.")

    if not (np.all(np.isfinite(K)) and np.all(np.isfinite(D)) and np.all(np.isfinite(H))):
        raise CalibrationError(f"Calibration matrices in '{calib_file}' contain NaN or Inf values.")
    if abs(np.linalg.det(H)) < 1e-12:
        raise CalibrationError(f"Homography matrix H in '{calib_file}' is singular.")

    camera_height_mm = (
        float(np.asarray(cal["camera_height_mm"]).item())
        if "camera_height_mm" in cal.files else 0.0
    )
    cap_height_mm = (
        float(np.asarray(cal["cap_height_mm"]).item())
        if "cap_height_mm" in cal.files else 13.0
    )
    if not (math.isfinite(camera_height_mm) and math.isfinite(cap_height_mm)):
        raise CalibrationError(f"Non-finite parallax heights in '{calib_file}'.")

    return {
        "K": K,
        "D": D,
        "H": H,
        "camera_type": cal_camera_type,
        "lens_model": lens_model,
        "image_size": cal_size,
        "camera_height_mm": camera_height_mm,
        "cap_height_mm": cap_height_mm,
    }


def _circle_intersects_rect(cx: float, cy: float, radius: float, x0: float, y0: float, x1: float, y1: float) -> bool:
    """Exact 2D circle vs axis-aligned rectangle intersection check."""
    nearest_x = min(max(cx, x0), x1)
    nearest_y = min(max(cy, y0), y1)
    dx = cx - nearest_x
    dy = cy - nearest_y
    return (dx * dx + dy * dy) < (radius * radius)

    def _estimate_px_per_mm(self) -> float:
        pts_mm = np.array([[[150.0, 150.0]], [[170.0, 150.0]], [[150.0, 170.0]]], dtype=np.float32)
        uv = cv2.perspectiveTransform(pts_mm, self.H_inv).reshape(3, 2)
        dx = float(np.linalg.norm(uv[1] - uv[0])) / 20.0
        dy = float(np.linalg.norm(uv[2] - uv[0])) / 20.0
        scale = 0.5 * (dx + dy)
        return scale if (math.isfinite(scale) and scale > 0.2) else 1.5

    def _estimate_optical_center_board_mm(self) -> tuple:
        cx_px, cy_px = float(self.K[0, 2]), float(self.K[1, 2])
        pt = np.array([[[cx_px, cy_px]]], dtype=np.float32)
        mapped = cv2.perspectiveTransform(pt, self.H)[0][0]
        bx, by = float(mapped[0]), float(mapped[1])
        if math.isfinite(bx) and math.isfinite(by) and -50.0 <= bx <= 350.0 and -50.0 <= by <= 350.0:
            return (bx, by)
        return (BOARD_SIZE_MM * 0.5, BOARD_SIZE_MM * 0.5)

    def apply_parallax_correction(self, x_mm: float, y_mm: float) -> tuple:
        """
        Compensates outward radial parallax caused by cap height (h_cap ~ 13 mm)
        relative to the Z=0 calibrated board plane when viewed from overhead H_cam.
        Disabled automatically if camera_height_mm <= cap_height_mm or <= 0.
        """
        if self.camera_height_mm > self.cap_height_mm > 0.0:
            scale = 1.0 - (self.cap_height_mm / self.camera_height_mm)
            cx, cy = self.cam_center_board_mm
            return (cx + (x_mm - cx) * scale, cy + (y_mm - cy) * scale)
        return (x_mm, y_mm)

    def undistort_frame(self, raw_bgr: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
        return cv2.remap(raw_bgr, self.map1, self.map2, interpolation=cv2.INTER_LINEAR)

    def capture_rectified_frame(self) -> np.ndarray:
        if self.picam2 is not None:
            raw = self.picam2.capture_array("main")
        elif self.usb_cam is not None:
            for _ in range(4):
                self.usb_cam.grab()
            ok, raw = self.usb_cam.read()
            if not ok:
                raise RuntimeError("USB camera frame acquisition failed")
        else:
            raise RuntimeError("No active camera hardware initialized")
        if raw.shape[1] != self.width or raw.shape[0] != self.height:
            raise CalibrationError(
                f"Captured frame size {(raw.shape[1], raw.shape[0])} does not match "
                f"calibrated resolution {(self.width, self.height)}."
            )
        return self.undistort_frame(raw)

    def _split_touching_cap_contour(self, cnt: np.ndarray, binary_mask: np.ndarray) -> list:
        """
        Separates merged contours of 2+ touching same-color caps (pairs, triangles,
        grids) using Euclidean distance transform + local peak extraction while
        preserving interior plywood gaps between touching caps.
        """
        x, y, w, h = cv2.boundingRect(cnt)
        pad = 2
        contour_roi = np.zeros((h + 2 * pad, w + 2 * pad), dtype=np.uint8)
        shifted = cnt - np.array([[[x - pad, y - pad]]], dtype=cnt.dtype)
        cv2.drawContours(contour_roi, [shifted], -1, 255, cv2.FILLED)

        # Intersect with actual binary_mask so interior gaps (e.g. center of 3 touching caps) remain 0
        roi = np.zeros_like(contour_roi)
        roi[pad:pad + h, pad:pad + w] = binary_mask[y:y + h, x:x + w]
        roi = cv2.bitwise_and(roi, contour_roi)

        dist = cv2.distanceTransform(roi, cv2.DIST_L2, 5)
        dist_smooth = cv2.GaussianBlur(dist, (5, 5), 1.0)

        min_peak_val = max(4.0, 0.45 * self.expected_cap_radius_px)
        k_size = max(5, int(round(self.expected_cap_radius_px * 0.55)) | 1)
        kernel = cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE, (k_size, k_size))
        dilated = cv2.dilate(dist_smooth, kernel)

        peak_mask = ((dist_smooth >= dilated - 1e-4) & (dist >= min_peak_val)).astype(np.uint8)
        num_labels, _, _, centroids = cv2.connectedComponentsWithStats(peak_mask)

        peaks = []
        for lbl in range(1, num_labels):
            px, py = float(centroids[lbl][0]), float(centroids[lbl][1])
            ix = min(max(int(round(px)), 0), dist.shape[1] - 1)
            iy = min(max(int(round(py)), 0), dist.shape[0] - 1)
            r_val = float(dist[iy, ix])
            if r_val >= min_peak_val:
                peaks.append((px + x - pad, py + y - pad, r_val))

        # Suppress secondary sub-peaks closer than 1.25 * expected_cap_radius_px
        peaks.sort(key=lambda p: p[2], reverse=True)
        min_sep_px = max(8.0, 1.25 * self.expected_cap_radius_px)
        kept = []
        for u, v, r_val in peaks:
            if any(math.hypot(u - ku, v - kv) < min_sep_px for ku, kv, _ in kept):
                continue
            kept.append((u, v, math.pi * r_val * r_val))
        return kept

    def deduplicate_spatial_nms(self, candidates: list) -> list:
        sorted_cands = sorted(candidates, key=lambda c: c.get("_area", 0.0), reverse=True)
        accepted = []
        for cand in sorted_cands:
            if any(
                math.hypot(cand["boardX_mm"] - a["boardX_mm"],
                           cand["boardY_mm"] - a["boardY_mm"]) < MIN_CAP_SEPARATION_MM
                for a in accepted
            ):
                continue
            item = {k: v for k, v in cand.items() if k != "_area"}
            item["capId"] = len(accepted) + 1
            accepted.append(item)
        return accepted[:MAX_BATCH_CAPS]

    def detect_caps(self, rectified_bgr: np.ndarray, occluded_y_mm: float = None) -> list:
        """
        Detects bottle caps at arbitrary continuous (X, Y) coordinates on the 300x300 mm
        board, splitting touching same-color clusters, compensating radial cap-height
        parallax, masking the gantry bridge band during bin-side scans, and tagging
        safe mechanical reachability (`in_safe_bounds`).
        """
        hsv = cv2.cvtColor(rectified_bgr, cv2.COLOR_BGR2HSV)
        candidates = []

        nominal_area = math.pi * (self.expected_cap_radius_px ** 2)
        min_cap_area = max(350.0, 0.32 * nominal_area)
        max_single_area = max(2400.0, 1.65 * nominal_area)

        for color_id, ranges in HSV_RANGES.items():
            mask = np.zeros((self.height, self.width), dtype=np.uint8)
            for lower, upper in ranges:
                mask |= cv2.inRange(hsv, np.array(lower, dtype=np.uint8), np.array(upper, dtype=np.uint8))

            mask_opened = cv2.morphologyEx(mask, cv2.MORPH_OPEN, self.kernel_open)
            mask_closed = cv2.morphologyEx(mask_opened, cv2.MORPH_CLOSE, self.kernel_close)
            contours, _ = cv2.findContours(mask_closed, cv2.RETR_EXTERNAL, cv2.CHAIN_APPROX_SIMPLE)

            for cnt in contours:
                area = cv2.contourArea(cnt)
                if area < min_cap_area:
                    continue
                perim = cv2.arcLength(cnt, True)
                circularity = (4.0 * math.pi * area) / (perim * perim + 1e-5)

                extracted = []
                if area <= max_single_area and circularity >= 0.72:
                    M = cv2.moments(cnt)
                    if M["m00"] > 0:
                        extracted.append((M["m10"] / M["m00"], M["m01"] / M["m00"], area))
                else:
                    # Merged same-color cluster or distorted silhouette -> distance-transform split
                    extracted.extend(self._split_touching_cap_contour(cnt, mask_opened))

                for u, v, est_area in extracted:
                    pt = np.array([[[u, v]]], dtype=np.float32)
                    board_pt = cv2.perspectiveTransform(pt, self.H)[0][0]
                    x_mm, y_mm = self.apply_parallax_correction(float(board_pt[0]), float(board_pt[1]))

                    if not (0.0 <= x_mm <= BOARD_SIZE_MM and 0.0 <= y_mm <= BOARD_SIZE_MM):
                        continue

                    # Ignore detections inside horizontal gantry bridge occlusion strip at bin
                    if (
                        occluded_y_mm is not None
                        and self.bridge_occlusion_half_mm > 0.0
                        and abs(y_mm - occluded_y_mm) <= self.bridge_occlusion_half_mm
                    ):
                        continue

                    in_safe = (
                        SAFE_PICK_MIN_X_MM <= x_mm <= SAFE_PICK_MAX_X_MM
                        and SAFE_PICK_MIN_Y_MM <= y_mm <= SAFE_PICK_MAX_Y_MM
                    )
                    _, _, bin_id = BIN_COORDS[color_id]
                    candidates.append({
                        "capId": 0,
                        "color": color_id,
                        "pixelX": int(round(u)),
                        "pixelY": int(round(v)),
                        "boardX_mm": x_mm,
                        "boardY_mm": y_mm,
                        "targetBinId": bin_id,
                        "in_safe_bounds": in_safe,
                        "_area": est_area,
                    })
        return self.deduplicate_spatial_nms(candidates)

    def count_jaw_collisions(self, target_cap: dict, all_caps: list) -> tuple:
        """
        Evaluates how many sides (0, 1, or 2) of the descending open parallel gripper
        jaws collide with neighboring caps along `self.jaw_axis`.
        Returns (blocked_sides, min_neighbor_dist_mm).
        """
        tx, ty = target_cap["boardX_mm"], target_cap["boardY_mm"]
        r_check = CAP_RADIUS_MM + JAW_CLEARANCE_PAD_MM
        neg_blocked = False
        pos_blocked = False
        min_dist = 1e9

        for other in all_caps:
            if other is target_cap:
                continue
            ox, oy = other["boardX_mm"], other["boardY_mm"]
            d = math.hypot(ox - tx, oy - ty)
            if d < 1e-3:
                continue
            if d < min_dist:
                min_dist = d

            if self.jaw_axis == "X":
                if not neg_blocked and _circle_intersects_rect(
                    ox, oy, r_check,
                    tx - JAW_OUTER_HALF_MM, ty - JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM,
                    tx - JAW_INNER_HALF_MM, ty + JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM,
                ):
                    neg_blocked = True
                if not pos_blocked and _circle_intersects_rect(
                    ox, oy, r_check,
                    tx + JAW_INNER_HALF_MM, ty - JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM,
                    tx + JAW_OUTER_HALF_MM, ty + JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM,
                ):
                    pos_blocked = True
            else:
                if not neg_blocked and _circle_intersects_rect(
                    ox, oy, r_check,
                    tx - JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM, ty - JAW_OUTER_HALF_MM,
                    tx + JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM, ty - JAW_INNER_HALF_MM,
                ):
                    neg_blocked = True
                if not pos_blocked and _circle_intersects_rect(
                    ox, oy, r_check,
                    tx - JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM, ty + JAW_INNER_HALF_MM,
                    tx + JAW_WIDTH_HALF_MM, ty + JAW_OUTER_HALF_MM,
                ):
                    pos_blocked = True

        return (int(neg_blocked) + int(pos_blocked), min_dist)

    def select_next_cap(
        self,
        detected_caps: list,
        cur_xy: tuple = (0.0, 0.0),
        retry_records: list = None,
        quarantined_zones: list = None,
    ):
        """
        Clearance-first / outermost-first pick selector:
          1. Excludes caps outside safe mechanical reach (`in_safe_bounds == False`)
             and quarantined unpickable spots, while STILL checking them as obstacles.
          2. Priority order: (retry_penalty, blocked_sides, travel_dist, -min_neighbor_dist).
             Picks completely clear caps first, peels clusters from outside in, and
             minimizes Cartesian travel from the current bin.
        """
        retry_records = retry_records or []
        quarantined_zones = quarantined_zones or []
        cur_x, cur_y = float(cur_xy[0]), float(cur_xy[1])

        scored = []
        for cap in detected_caps:
            if not cap.get("in_safe_bounds", True):
                continue
            cx, cy, col = cap["boardX_mm"], cap["boardY_mm"], cap["color"]
            if any(
                q["color"] == col and math.hypot(cx - q["x"], cy - q["y"]) <= MISSED_PICK_RADIUS_MM
                for q in quarantined_zones
            ):
                continue

            retry_penalty = 1 if any(
                r["color"] == col and r["count"] > 0
                and math.hypot(cx - r["x"], cy - r["y"]) <= MISSED_PICK_RADIUS_MM
                for r in retry_records
            ) else 0

            blocked_sides, min_neighbor = self.count_jaw_collisions(cap, detected_caps)
            travel = math.hypot(cx - cur_x, cy - cur_y)
            scored.append(((retry_penalty, blocked_sides, travel, -min_neighbor), blocked_sides, cap))

        if not scored:
            return None

        scored.sort(key=lambda item: item[0])
        _, blocked_sides, best = scored[0]
        chosen = dict(best)
        chosen["blocked_sides"] = blocked_sides
        return chosen

    def _update_retry_state(
        self,
        last_target: dict,
        detected_caps: list,
        retry_records: list,
        quarantined_zones: list,
        occluded_y_mm: float = None,
    ) -> bool:
        """
        Checks whether `last_target` is still sitting at its previous location on an
        unoccluded region of the fresh scan. Quarantines spots after MAX_PICK_RETRIES.
        Returns True if `last_target` was visibly evaluated (not occluded), False otherwise.
        """
        if last_target is None:
            return False
        tx, ty, col = last_target["boardX_mm"], last_target["boardY_mm"], last_target["color"]

        # Do not judge pick outcome while last_target sits inside the masked bridge strip
        if (
            occluded_y_mm is not None
            and self.bridge_occlusion_half_mm > 0.0
            and abs(ty - occluded_y_mm) <= self.bridge_occlusion_half_mm
        ):
            return False

        already_quarantined = any(
            q["color"] == col and math.hypot(q["x"] - tx, q["y"] - ty) <= MISSED_PICK_RADIUS_MM
            for q in quarantined_zones
        )
        if already_quarantined:
            return True

        still_present = any(
            c["color"] == col and math.hypot(c["boardX_mm"] - tx, c["boardY_mm"] - ty) <= MISSED_PICK_RADIUS_MM
            for c in detected_caps
        )

        rec = next(
            (r for r in retry_records
             if r["color"] == col and math.hypot(r["x"] - tx, r["y"] - ty) <= MISSED_PICK_RADIUS_MM),
            None,
        )

        if still_present:
            if rec is None:
                rec = {"x": tx, "y": ty, "color": col, "count": 1}
                retry_records.append(rec)
            else:
                rec["x"], rec["y"] = tx, ty
                rec["count"] += 1
            if rec["count"] >= MAX_PICK_RETRIES:
                quarantined_zones.append({"x": tx, "y": ty, "color": col, "attempts": rec["count"]})
                print(
                    f"[WARN] Cap (color={col}) at ({tx:.1f}, {ty:.1f}) mm still present after "
                    f"{rec['count']} attempts; quarantining spot for this session.",
                    file=sys.stderr,
                )
        elif rec is not None:
            retry_records.remove(rec)
        return True

    def optimize_path(self, caps: list) -> list:
        """Legacy greedy path optimizer (retained for offline tools & unit tests)."""
        ordered, remaining = [], [dict(c) for c in caps]
        cur_x, cur_y = 0.0, 0.0
        seq_id = 1
        while remaining:
            remaining.sort(key=lambda c: math.hypot(c["boardX_mm"] - cur_x, c["boardY_mm"] - cur_y))
            nxt = remaining.pop(0)
            nxt["capId"] = seq_id
            seq_id += 1
            ordered.append(nxt)
            cur_x, cur_y, _ = BIN_COORDS[nxt["color"]]
        return ordered

    def pack_single_target_packet(self, cap: dict) -> bytes:
        """
        Packs an 18-byte CRC16-verified closed-loop single-target packet:
          [0x5B][CapTarget: <BBHHffB (15B)][CRC16: <H (2B)]
        """
        payload = struct.pack(
            "<BBHHffB",
            int(cap.get("capId", 1)) & 0xFF,
            int(cap["color"]) & 0xFF,
            int(cap["pixelX"]) & 0xFFFF,
            int(cap["pixelY"]) & 0xFFFF,
            float(cap["boardX_mm"]),
            float(cap["boardY_mm"]),
            int(cap["targetBinId"]) & 0xFF,
        )
        crc = crc16_ccitt(payload)
        return bytes([PROTOCOL_SINGLE_MAGIC]) + payload + struct.pack("<H", crc)

    def pack_batch_packet(self, ordered_caps: list) -> bytes:
        if not (1 <= len(ordered_caps) <= MAX_BATCH_CAPS):
            raise ValueError(f"Batch size must be in 1..{MAX_BATCH_CAPS}, got {len(ordered_caps)}")
        payload = bytearray()
        for c in ordered_caps:
            # Matches struct CapTarget: <BBHHffB (15 bytes packed)
            payload += struct.pack(
                "<BBHHffB",
                c["capId"], c["color"], c["pixelX"], c["pixelY"],
                c["boardX_mm"], c["boardY_mm"], c["targetBinId"],
            )
        crc = crc16_ccitt(bytes(payload))
        # Matches struct BatchHeader: <BHBH (6 bytes packed)
        header = struct.pack("<BHBH", PROTOCOL_MAGIC_BYTE, self.batch_id, len(ordered_caps), crc)
        self.batch_id = ((self.batch_id + 1) & 0xFFFF) or 1
        return header + bytes(payload)

    def write_paced_uart(self, packet: bytes, chunk_size: int = 32, sleep_fn=time.sleep):
        # Pace in 32-byte chunks with 2 ms pause so Mega's 64-byte RX ring buffer never overflows
        for offset in range(0, len(packet), chunk_size):
            self.uart.write(packet[offset:offset + chunk_size])
            self.uart.flush()
            if offset + chunk_size < len(packet):
                sleep_fn(0.002)

    def wait_for_mega_ready(
        self,
        timeout_s: float = None,
        poll_interval_s: float = READY_POLL_INTERVAL_S,
        clock=time.monotonic,
        sleep_fn=time.sleep,
    ) -> bool:
        """
        Explicit READY/BUSY/FAULT startup handshake. Never transmits motion data
        until Mega reports CMD_STATUS_READY after homing finishes.
        """
        if self.uart is None:
            return False

        start_t = clock()
        last_poll_t = -1e9
        while timeout_s is None or (clock() - start_t < timeout_s):
            now = clock()
            if now - last_poll_t >= poll_interval_s:
                self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_QUERY_STATUS]))
                self.uart.flush()
                last_poll_t = now

            while self.uart.in_waiting > 0:
                resp = self.uart.read(1)[0]
                if resp == CMD_STATUS_READY:
                    return True
                if resp in (CMD_STATUS_FAULT, CMD_HARD_FAULT, CMD_RECOVERY_HALT):
                    print(
                        f"[FAULT] Mega reported status 0x{resp:02X}; "
                        "motion inhibited until operator clears fault & re-arms.",
                        file=sys.stderr,
                    )
                    return False
                # CMD_STATUS_BUSY: keep polling until Mega finishes homing

            sleep_fn(0.02)
        return False

    def _send_single_target_with_ack(self, cap: dict, sleep_fn=time.sleep) -> str:
        packet = self.pack_single_target_packet(cap)
        for _ in range(3):
            self.uart.reset_input_buffer()
            self.uart.write(packet)
            self.uart.flush()
            ack = self.uart.read(1)
            if ack:
                code = ack[0]
                if code == CMD_BATCH_ACK:
                    return "ACK"
                if code in (CMD_HARD_FAULT, CMD_RECOVERY_HALT, CMD_STATUS_FAULT):
                    return "FAULT"
            sleep_fn(0.05)
        return "NACK"

    def _await_opcode_with_heartbeat(
        self,
        target_opcode: int,
        timeout_s: float = MAX_SINGLE_CAP_S,
        clock=time.monotonic,
        sleep_fn=time.sleep,
    ) -> str:
        start_t = clock()
        while True:
            self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING]))
            self.uart.flush()
            sleep_fn(HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_S)

            while self.uart.in_waiting > 0:
                resp = self.uart.read(1)[0]
                if resp == target_opcode:
                    return "OK"
                if resp in (CMD_RECOVERY_HALT, CMD_HARD_FAULT, CMD_STATUS_FAULT):
                    return "FAULT"

            if clock() - start_t > timeout_s:
                return "TIMEOUT"

    def run_closed_loop_sort_session(
        self,
        capture_fn=None,
        clock=time.monotonic,
        sleep_fn=time.sleep,
        max_cycles: int = MAX_SESSION_CYCLES,
    ) -> dict:
        """
        Executes a full closed-loop sorting session:
          1. Captures Scan #0 at unobstructed (0, 0).
          2. Sends one clearance-ranked CapTarget at a time via CMD_PICK_SINGLE (0x5B).
          3. On Mega CMD_SCAN_WINDOW_OPEN (0xA5) at the color bin, captures & analyzes
             the fresh board IN PARALLEL with the bin drop, updating bumped coordinates
             and missed-pick retry/quarantine state.
          4. On CMD_AWAIT_NEXT (0xA6), immediately dispatches the next cap from the bin,
             or executes CMD_PARK_FOR_SCAN (0xB5) for an unobstructed clear-FOV check
             before terminating cleanly with CMD_FINISH_SESSION (0xB6).
        """
        if self.uart is None:
            return {"status": "NACK", "picked": 0, "scans": 0, "quarantined": [], "out_of_bounds_seen": 0}

        cap_source = capture_fn or self.capture_rectified_frame
        retry_records = []
        quarantined_zones = []
        scans = 0
        picked = 0
        out_of_bounds_seen = 0

        # Scan #0: Unobstructed full-board scan while parked at (0, 0)
        frame = cap_source()
        scans += 1
        caps = self.detect_caps(frame, occluded_y_mm=None)
        out_of_bounds_seen += sum(1 for c in caps if not c.get("in_safe_bounds", True))
        next_cap = self.select_next_cap(caps, (0.0, 0.0), retry_records, quarantined_zones)

        if next_cap is None:
            return {
                "status": "COMPLETE",
                "picked": 0,
                "scans": scans,
                "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
                "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
            }

        for _ in range(max_cycles):
            ack_status = self._send_single_target_with_ack(next_cap, sleep_fn=sleep_fn)
            if ack_status != "ACK":
                return {
                    "status": ack_status,
                    "picked": picked,
                    "scans": scans,
                    "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
                    "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
                }

            last_target = next_cap
            bin_x, bin_y, _ = BIN_COORDS[last_target["color"]]
            cycle_start = clock()
            scanned_at_bin = False
            retry_evaluated = False
            await_received = False
            candidate_next = None

            while not await_received:
                self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING]))
                self.uart.flush()
                sleep_fn(HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_S)

                while self.uart.in_waiting > 0:
                    resp = self.uart.read(1)[0]
                    if resp == CMD_SCAN_WINDOW_OPEN and not scanned_at_bin:
                        # Pipelined capture while Mega lowers/drops/retracts at color bin
                        fresh = cap_source()
                        scans += 1
                        scanned_at_bin = True
                        self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING]))
                        self.uart.flush()

                        fresh_caps = self.detect_caps(fresh, occluded_y_mm=bin_y)
                        out_of_bounds_seen += sum(1 for c in fresh_caps if not c.get("in_safe_bounds", True))
                        retry_evaluated = self._update_retry_state(
                            last_target, fresh_caps, retry_records, quarantined_zones, occluded_y_mm=bin_y
                        )
                        candidate_next = self.select_next_cap(
                            fresh_caps, (bin_x, bin_y), retry_records, quarantined_zones
                        )
                    elif resp == CMD_CAP_PROGRESS:
                        cycle_start = clock()
                    elif resp == CMD_AWAIT_NEXT:
                        await_received = True
                        break
                    elif resp in (CMD_RECOVERY_HALT, CMD_HARD_FAULT, CMD_STATUS_FAULT):
                        return {
                            "status": "FAULT",
                            "picked": picked,
                            "scans": scans,
                            "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
                            "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
                        }

                if not await_received and (clock() - cycle_start > MAX_SINGLE_CAP_S):
                    print("[TIMEOUT] Mega stopped responding during closed-loop cap cycle.", file=sys.stderr)
                    return {
                        "status": "TIMEOUT",
                        "picked": picked,
                        "scans": scans,
                        "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
                        "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
                    }

            picked += 1

            # Fallback capture if Mega signaled AWAIT_NEXT without prior SCAN_WINDOW_OPEN
            if not scanned_at_bin:
                fresh = cap_source()
                scans += 1
                self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING]))
                self.uart.flush()
                fresh_caps = self.detect_caps(fresh, occluded_y_mm=bin_y)
                out_of_bounds_seen += sum(1 for c in fresh_caps if not c.get("in_safe_bounds", True))
                retry_evaluated = self._update_retry_state(
                    last_target, fresh_caps, retry_records, quarantined_zones, occluded_y_mm=bin_y
                )
                candidate_next = self.select_next_cap(
                    fresh_caps, (bin_x, bin_y), retry_records, quarantined_zones
                )

            if candidate_next is not None:
                next_cap = candidate_next
                continue

            # 0 graspable caps visible outside bridge band -> clear-FOV verification park at (0,0)
            self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_PARK_FOR_SCAN]))
            self.uart.flush()
            park_res = self._await_opcode_with_heartbeat(
                CMD_AWAIT_NEXT, timeout_s=MAX_SINGLE_CAP_S, clock=clock, sleep_fn=sleep_fn
            )
            if park_res != "OK":
                return {
                    "status": park_res,
                    "picked": picked,
                    "scans": scans,
                    "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
                    "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
                }

            verify_frame = cap_source()
            scans += 1
            self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING]))
            self.uart.flush()
            verify_caps = self.detect_caps(verify_frame, occluded_y_mm=None)
            out_of_bounds_seen += sum(1 for c in verify_caps if not c.get("in_safe_bounds", True))
            if not retry_evaluated:
                self._update_retry_state(
                    last_target, verify_caps, retry_records, quarantined_zones, occluded_y_mm=None
                )
            revealed = self.select_next_cap(
                verify_caps, (0.0, 0.0), retry_records, quarantined_zones
            )
            if revealed is not None:
                next_cap = revealed
                continue

            # Confirmed empty (or only quarantined/out-of-bounds caps remain) -> finish cleanly
            self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_FINISH_SESSION]))
            self.uart.flush()
            done_res = self._await_opcode_with_heartbeat(
                CMD_BATCH_COMPLETE, timeout_s=MAX_SINGLE_CAP_S, clock=clock, sleep_fn=sleep_fn
            )
            return {
                "status": "COMPLETE" if done_res == "OK" else done_res,
                "picked": picked,
                "scans": scans,
                "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
                "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
            }

        # Reached hard cycle limit -> park safely
        self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_FINISH_SESSION]))
        self.uart.flush()
        self._await_opcode_with_heartbeat(
            CMD_BATCH_COMPLETE, timeout_s=MAX_SINGLE_CAP_S, clock=clock, sleep_fn=sleep_fn
        )
        return {
            "status": "MAX_CYCLES",
            "picked": picked,
            "scans": scans,
            "quarantined": quarantined_zones,
            "out_of_bounds_seen": out_of_bounds_seen,
        }

    def transmit_batch_and_supervise(
        self,
        ordered_caps: list,
        clock=time.monotonic,
        sleep_fn=time.sleep,
    ) -> str:
        """
        Legacy open-loop batch transmitter (retained for offline tools & protocol tests).
        Transmits a CRC16-verified batch and maintains 250 ms heartbeat pings
        for the entire batch duration.

        Returns one of: "COMPLETE", "NACK", "FAULT", "TIMEOUT".
        """
        if not ordered_caps or self.uart is None:
            return "NACK"
        packet = self.pack_batch_packet(ordered_caps)

        ack_received = False
        for _ in range(3):
            self.uart.reset_input_buffer()
            self.write_paced_uart(packet, sleep_fn=sleep_fn)
            ack = self.uart.read(1)
            if ack:
                code = ack[0]
                if code == CMD_BATCH_ACK:
                    ack_received = True
                    break
                if code in (CMD_HARD_FAULT, CMD_RECOVERY_HALT, CMD_STATUS_FAULT):
                    return "FAULT"
            sleep_fn(0.05)

        if not ack_received:
            return "NACK"

        start_t = clock()
        last_progress_t = start_t
        max_total_s = len(ordered_caps) * PER_CAP_BUDGET_S + BATCH_OVERHEAD_S

        while True:
            self.uart.write(bytes([CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING]))
            self.uart.flush()
            sleep_fn(HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_S)

            while self.uart.in_waiting > 0:
                resp = self.uart.read(1)[0]
                if resp == CMD_CAP_PROGRESS:
                    last_progress_t = clock()
                elif resp == CMD_BATCH_COMPLETE:
                    return "COMPLETE"
                elif resp in (CMD_RECOVERY_HALT, CMD_HARD_FAULT, CMD_STATUS_FAULT):
                    return "FAULT"

            now = clock()
            if (now - last_progress_t > MAX_SINGLE_CAP_S) or (now - start_t > max_total_s):
                print(
                    "[TIMEOUT] Mega stopped reporting progress or exceeded dynamic batch ceiling.",
                    file=sys.stderr,
                )
                return "TIMEOUT"


def main():
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description="CapSorter V2 Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W Closed-Loop Vision Controller")
    parser.add_argument("--calib", help="Path to validated camera calibration .npz file")
    parser.add_argument("--camera-type", choices=("picamera2_imx219", "usb_v4l2"))
    parser.add_argument("--serial-port", default="/dev/serial0")
    parser.add_argument("--bridge-occlusion-mm", type=float, default=BRIDGE_OCCLUSION_HALF_MM)
    parser.add_argument("--jaw-axis", choices=("X", "Y"), default=GRIPPER_JAW_AXIS)
    args = parser.parse_args()

    try:
        controller = PiZeroVisionController(
            calib_file=args.calib,
            serial_port=args.serial_port,
            camera_type=args.camera_type,
            init_hardware=True,
            bridge_occlusion_half_mm=args.bridge_occlusion_mm,
            jaw_axis=args.jaw_axis,
        )
    except CalibrationError as exc:
        print(f"[FAIL-CLOSED CALIBRATION ERROR] {exc}", file=sys.stderr)
        sys.exit(2)

    print("[V2] Waiting for Arduino Mega CMD_STATUS_READY handshake...")
    while not controller.wait_for_mega_ready(timeout_s=5.0):
        print("[V2] Mega not READY (homing or fault latched); retrying status query...")
        time.sleep(1.0)
    print("[V2] Mega READY. Starting closed-loop bin-side re-imaging vision loop.")

    while True:
        if not controller.wait_for_mega_ready(timeout_s=2.0):
            print("[V2] Mega unavailable or faulted; waiting for operator re-arm & READY...")
            time.sleep(1.0)
            continue

        result = controller.run_closed_loop_sort_session()
        if result["status"] != "COMPLETE":
            print(
                f"[V2] Session ended with status={result['status']} "
                f"(picked={result['picked']}, scans={result['scans']}). "
                "Inhibiting new scans until Mega is READY.",
                file=sys.stderr,
            )
            time.sleep(1.0)
        elif result["picked"] > 0:
            print(
                f"[V2] Session COMPLETE: picked={result['picked']}, scans={result['scans']}, "
                f"quarantined={len(result['quarantined'])}."
            )
        time.sleep(0.5)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

import cv2
import numpy as np

VALID_CAMERA_TYPES = ("picamera2_imx219", "usb_v4l2")
VALID_LENS_MODELS = ("fisheye", "pinhole")

# Physical 300x300 mm Sorting Board Fiducial Corners (in millimeters)
BOARD_CORNERS_MM = np.array([
    [0.0,   0.0],     # Top-Left Marker (Origin)
    [300.0, 0.0],     # Top-Right Marker
    [300.0, 300.0],   # Bottom-Right Marker
    [0.0,   300.0],   # Bottom-Left Marker
], dtype=np.float32)


def calibrate_intrinsics_from_checkerboards(
    image_paths: list,
    pattern_size: tuple = (9, 6),
    square_size_mm: float = 25.0,
    lens_model: str = "fisheye",
    min_images: int = 8,
    max_reproj_err: float = 1.0,
):
    """
    Computes camera intrinsic matrix K and distortion coefficients D from
    real checkerboard images using either OpenCV's fisheye or pinhole model.
    Returns (K, D, image_size, rms_error_px).
    """
    if lens_model not in VALID_LENS_MODELS:
        raise ValueError(f"Unsupported lens_model '{lens_model}'; expected one of {VALID_LENS_MODELS}")
    if not image_paths:
        raise ValueError("No checkerboard images provided for intrinsic calibration.")

    cols, rows = int(pattern_size[0]), int(pattern_size[1])
    objp = np.zeros((1, cols * rows, 3), np.float32)
    objp[0, :, :2] = np.mgrid[0:cols, 0:rows].T.reshape(-1, 2) * float(square_size_mm)

    objpoints = []
    imgpoints = []
    image_size = None
    subpix_criteria = (cv2.TERM_CRITERIA_EPS + cv2.TERM_CRITERIA_MAX_ITER, 30, 1e-3)

    for path in sorted(image_paths):
        img = cv2.imread(path)
        if img is None:
            continue
        gray = cv2.cvtColor(img, cv2.COLOR_BGR2GRAY)
        h, w = gray.shape[:2]
        if image_size is None:
            image_size = (w, h)
        elif image_size != (w, h):
            raise ValueError(
                f"Inconsistent image resolution in {path}: got {(w, h)}, expected {image_size}"
            )

        found, corners = cv2.findChessboardCorners(
            gray, (cols, rows),
            cv2.CALIB_CB_ADAPTIVE_THRESH + cv2.CALIB_CB_FAST_CHECK + cv2.CALIB_CB_NORMALIZE_IMAGE,
        )
        if not found:
            continue

        refined = cv2.cornerSubPix(gray, corners, (11, 11), (-1, -1), subpix_criteria)
        if lens_model == "fisheye":
            objpoints.append(objp.astype(np.float64))
            imgpoints.append(refined.reshape(1, -1, 2).astype(np.float64))
        else:
            objpoints.append(objp.reshape(-1, 3).astype(np.float32))
            imgpoints.append(refined.astype(np.float32))

    if len(objpoints) < min_images:
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"Only {len(objpoints)} valid checkerboard images found; need at least {min_images}."
        )

    if lens_model == "fisheye":
        K = np.zeros((3, 3), dtype=np.float64)
        D = np.zeros((4, 1), dtype=np.float64)
        rvecs = [np.zeros((1, 1, 3), dtype=np.float64) for _ in objpoints]
        tvecs = [np.zeros((1, 1, 3), dtype=np.float64) for _ in objpoints]
        flags = (
            cv2.fisheye.CALIB_RECOMPUTE_EXTRINSIC
            + cv2.fisheye.CALIB_FIX_SKEW
        )
        rms, K, D, _, _ = cv2.fisheye.calibrate(
            objpoints, imgpoints, image_size, K, D, rvecs, tvecs,
            flags, (cv2.TERM_CRITERIA_EPS + cv2.TERM_CRITERIA_MAX_ITER, 100, 1e-6),
        )
        D = np.asarray(D, dtype=np.float64).reshape((4, 1))
    else:
        rms, K, D, _, _ = cv2.calibrateCamera(
            objpoints, imgpoints, image_size, None, None
        )
        K = np.asarray(K, dtype=np.float64).reshape((3, 3))
        D = np.asarray(D, dtype=np.float64).reshape((-1, 1))

    if not np.isfinite(rms) or rms > max_reproj_err:
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"Intrinsic calibration RMS reprojection error {rms:.4f} px exceeds limit {max_reproj_err:.4f} px."
        )

    return K, D, image_size, float(rms)


def compute_homography_from_fiducials(
    rectified_pixel_corners: np.ndarray,
    max_corner_err_mm: float = 1.0,
) -> np.ndarray:
    """
    Computes 3x3 planar homography matrix H mapping rectified image pixels
    (u, v) to physical sorting board coordinates (X_mm, Y_mm) and validates
    corner round-trip accuracy.
    """
    rect_pts = np.asarray(rectified_pixel_corners, dtype=np.float32).reshape((4, 2))
    if not np.all(np.isfinite(rect_pts)):
        raise ValueError("Rectified fiducial pixel corners contain non-finite values.")

    H, _ = cv2.findHomography(rect_pts, BOARD_CORNERS_MM, method=0)
    if H is None or not np.all(np.isfinite(H)) or abs(np.linalg.det(H)) < 1e-12:
        raise ValueError("Degenerate fiducial corners produced a singular homography matrix.")

    H = H.astype(np.float64)
    mapped = cv2.perspectiveTransform(rect_pts.reshape(1, 4, 2), H).reshape(4, 2)
    max_err = float(np.max(np.linalg.norm(mapped - BOARD_CORNERS_MM, axis=1)))
    if max_err > max_corner_err_mm:
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"Homography corner mapping error {max_err:.4f} mm exceeds limit {max_corner_err_mm:.4f} mm."
        )
    return H

    if lens_model not in VALID_LENS_MODELS:
        raise ValueError(f"Unsupported lens_model '{lens_model}'")

    if out_dir:
        os.makedirs(out_dir, exist_ok=True)

    np.savez(
        out_path,
        K=np.asarray(K, dtype=np.float64).reshape((3, 3)),
        D=np.asarray(D, dtype=np.float64).reshape((-1, 1)),
        H=np.asarray(H, dtype=np.float64).reshape((3, 3)),
        camera_type=np.array(camera_type),
        lens_model=np.array(lens_model),
        image_size=np.array([int(image_size[0]), int(image_size[1])], dtype=np.int32),

        calibrated_utc=np.array(timestamp),
    )

        f"[OK] Saved validated calibration ({camera_type}, {lens_model}, "
        f"{image_size[0]}x{image_size[1]}, RMS={reprojection_error_px:.4f} px, "
        f"H_cam={camera_height_mm:.1f} mm, h_cap={cap_height_mm:.1f} mm) -> {out_path}"
    )


def save_uncalibrated_placeholder(out_path: str):
    """Writes an explicitly uncalibrated placeholder so runtime fails closed until bench calibrated."""
    out_dir = os.path.dirname(out_path)
    if out_dir:
        os.makedirs(out_dir, exist_ok=True)
    np.savez(
        out_path,
        K=np.eye(3, dtype=np.float64),
        D=np.zeros((4, 1), dtype=np.float64),
        H=np.eye(3, dtype=np.float64),
        camera_type=np.array("uncalibrated_placeholder"),
        lens_model=np.array("uncalibrated"),
        image_size=np.array([0, 0], dtype=np.int32),
        reprojection_error_px=np.array(float("nan"), dtype=np.float64),
        calibrated=np.array(False, dtype=bool),
        calibrated_utc=np.array(""),
    )


def _parse_corners_arg(corners_str: str) -> np.ndarray:
    pairs = [p.strip() for p in corners_str.split(";") if p.strip()]
    if len(pairs) != 4:
        raise ValueError("Expected 4 semicolon-separated 'u,v' corners (TL;TR;BR;BL).")
    pts = []
    for p in pairs:
        u_str, v_str = p.split(",")
        pts.append([float(u_str.strip()), float(v_str.strip())])
    return np.array(pts, dtype=np.float32)


def main():
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(
        description="Calibrate V2 camera intrinsics (checkerboard) and 4-corner board homography."
    )
    parser.add_argument("--camera-type", required=True, choices=VALID_CAMERA_TYPES)
    parser.add_argument("--lens-model", required=True, choices=VALID_LENS_MODELS)
    parser.add_argument(
        "--checkerboard-glob", required=True,
        help="Glob pattern of checkerboard calibration images (e.g. 'calib_imgs/*.png')",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--pattern", default="9x6",
        help="Inner checkerboard corner counts as COLSxROWS (default: 9x6)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--square-mm", type=float, default=25.0,
        help="Physical checkerboard square edge length in mm (default: 25.0)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--corners",
        help="Rectified board fiducial corners as 'u0,v0;u1,v1;u2,v2;u3,v3' (TL;TR;BR;BL)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--corners-json",
        help="Path to JSON file containing a 4x2 list of rectified fiducial pixel corners",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--min-images", type=int, default=8,
        help="Minimum valid checkerboard images required (default: 8)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--max-reproj-err", type=float, default=1.0,
        help="Maximum allowed RMS reprojection error in pixels (default: 1.0)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--camera-height-mm", type=float, default=0.0,
        help="Measured overhead camera lens height above board plane in mm for radial parallax correction (0 disables)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--cap-height-mm", type=float, default=13.0,
        help="Nominal bottle-cap height in mm used with --camera-height-mm parallax compensation (default: 13.0)",
    )
    parser.add_argument(
        "--out",
        help="Output .npz path (defaults to calibration_csi_imx219.npz or calibration_usb.npz)",
    )
    args = parser.parse_args()

    if bool(args.corners) == bool(args.corners_json):
        parser.error("Provide exactly one of --corners or --corners-json with measured fiducials.")

    cols, rows = (int(x) for x in args.pattern.lower().split("x"))
    image_paths = sorted(glob.glob(args.checkerboard_glob))
    K, D, image_size, rms = calibrate_intrinsics_from_checkerboards(
        image_paths=image_paths,
        pattern_size=(cols, rows),
        square_size_mm=args.square_mm,
        lens_model=args.lens_model,
        min_images=args.min_images,
        max_reproj_err=args.max_reproj_err,
    )

    if args.corners:
        corners_px = _parse_corners_arg(args.corners)
    else:
        with open(args.corners_json, "r", encoding="utf-8") as fh:
            corners_px = np.array(json.load(fh), dtype=np.float32)

    H = compute_homography_from_fiducials(corners_px)
    default_out = (
        "calibration_csi_imx219.npz"
        if args.camera_type == "picamera2_imx219"
        else "calibration_usb.npz"
    )
    save_validated_calibration(
        out_path=args.out or default_out,
        K=K, D=D, H=H,
        camera_type=args.camera_type,
        lens_model=args.lens_model,
        image_size=image_size,
        reprojection_error_px=rms,
        camera_height_mm=args.camera_height_mm,
        cap_height_mm=args.cap_height_mm,
    )


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

}

#if HAS_GRIP_SENSOR
  return hasInFlightCap && (digitalRead(PIN_GRIP_SENSE) == LOW);

  return false;

}

bool isValidSingleTarget(const CapTarget &cap) {
  if (!isfinite(cap.boardX_mm) || !isfinite(cap.boardY_mm)) return false;
  if (cap.boardX_mm < SAFE_PICK_MIN_X_MM || cap.boardX_mm > SAFE_PICK_MAX_X_MM) return false;
  if (cap.boardY_mm < SAFE_PICK_MIN_Y_MM || cap.boardY_mm > SAFE_PICK_MAX_Y_MM) return false;
  if (cap.targetBinId < 1 || cap.targetBinId > 4) return false;
  return true;
}

void moveGantryToMm(float boardX_mm, float boardY_mm) {
  float targetX = constrain(boardX_mm + TOOL_OFFSET_X_MM, 0.0f, MAX_TRAVEL_X_MM);
  float targetY = constrain(boardY_mm + TOOL_OFFSET_Y_MM, 0.0f, MAX_TRAVEL_Y_MM);
  stepperX.moveTo(lroundf(targetX * STEPS_PER_MM_X));
  stepperY.moveTo(lroundf(targetY * STEPS_PER_MM_Y));
}

bool isGantryAtTarget() {
  return (stepperX.distanceToGo() == 0 && stepperY.distanceToGo() == 0);
}

void replyStatusQuery() {
  if (currentState == STATE_IDLE && isHomed && !commsFaultLatched && !isEstopActive()) {
    Serial1.write(CMD_STATUS_READY);
  } else if (currentState == STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT || currentState == STATE_HARD_FAULT) {
    Serial1.write(CMD_STATUS_FAULT);
  } else {
    Serial1.write(CMD_STATUS_BUSY);
  }
}

void serviceSerialDuringHoming() {
  // Drain Serial1 non-blockingly while homing so host queries get BUSY and FIFO never stalls
  while (Serial1.available() > 0) {
    uint8_t b = (uint8_t)Serial1.read();
    if (b == CMD_QUERY_STATUS) {
      Serial1.write(CMD_STATUS_BUSY);
    } else if (b == PROTOCOL_MAGIC_BYTE || b == PROTOCOL_SINGLE_MAGIC) {
      Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
    }
  }
}

void triggerHardFault(const __FlashStringHelper *reason) {
  stepperX.setSpeed(0.0f);
  stepperY.setSpeed(0.0f);
  stepperX.moveTo(stepperX.currentPosition());
  stepperY.moveTo(stepperY.currentPosition());
  digitalWrite(PIN_STEPPER_EN, HIGH); // Disable stepper coils immediately
  digitalWrite(PIN_SERVO_OE, HIGH);   // Disable PCA9685 PWM outputs (no commanded fault motion)
  closedLoopMode    = false;
  isHomed           = false;
  hasInFlightCap    = false;
  batchTotal        = 0;
  currentIdx        = 0;
  currentState      = STATE_HARD_FAULT;
  Serial1.write(CMD_HARD_FAULT);
  Serial.print(F("[HARD_FAULT] "));
  Serial.println(reason);
}

bool waitServicedDelayMs(unsigned long durationMs) {
  unsigned long start = millis();
  while (millis() - start < durationMs) {
    if (isEstopActive()) {
      triggerHardFault(F("E-Stop active during servo settle"));
      return false;
    }
    serviceSerialDuringHoming();
  }
  return true;
}

void homeAxes() {
  currentState      = STATE_HOMING;
  closedLoopMode    = false;
  isHomed           = false;
  hasInFlightCap    = false;
  commsFaultLatched = false;
  limitFaultCount   = 0;
  estopFaultCount   = 0;
  batchTotal        = 0;
  currentIdx        = 0;

  // 0. Refuse homing if hardwired E-stop auxiliary contact is active or Z overtravel is open
  if (isEstopActive()) {
    triggerHardFault(F("E-Stop active at homing start"));
    return;
  }
#if Z_LIMIT_IS_OVERTRAVEL
  if (isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Z_HOME)) {
    triggerHardFault(F("Z overtravel switch open or broken wire at homing start"));
    return;
  }
#endif

  // 1. Retract Z-axis and open gripper cleanly before energizing XY steppers
  pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_RAISED);
  pca.setPWM(SERVO_GRIPPER_CH, 0, PWM_GRIP_OPEN);
  digitalWrite(PIN_SERVO_OE, LOW);    // Enable PCA9685 outputs after latching safe PWM
  if (!waitServicedDelayMs(450UL)) return;

  if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
    triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis did not confirm retracted safe state before XY homing"));
    return;
  }

  digitalWrite(PIN_STEPPER_EN, LOW);  // Enable TMC2208 drivers (Active LOW)
  stepperX.setMinPulseWidth(2);
  stepperY.setMinPulseWidth(2);
  stepperX.setMaxSpeed(1000.0f);
  stepperY.setMaxSpeed(1000.0f);
  stepperX.setAcceleration(800.0f);
  stepperY.setAcceleration(800.0f);

  // 2. Broken NC Wire Pre-Check: Back off ONLY the axis whose switch already reads HIGH
  bool xInitiallyHigh = isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_X_HOME);
  bool yInitiallyHigh = isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Y_HOME);
  if (xInitiallyHigh || yInitiallyHigh) {
    stepperX.setCurrentPosition(0);
    stepperY.setCurrentPosition(0);
    stepperX.moveTo(xInitiallyHigh ? lroundf(10.0f * STEPS_PER_MM_X) : 0L);
    stepperY.moveTo(yInitiallyHigh ? lroundf(10.0f * STEPS_PER_MM_Y) : 0L);
    unsigned long preStart = millis();
    while (!isGantryAtTarget()) {
      if (isEstopActive()) {
        triggerHardFault(F("E-Stop triggered during pre-homing backoff"));
        return;
      }
      if (millis() - preStart > BACKOFF_TIMEOUT_MS) {
        triggerHardFault(F("Pre-homing backoff timeout"));
        return;
      }
      stepperX.run();
      stepperY.run();
      serviceSerialDuringHoming();
    }
    // If switch STILL reads HIGH after +10 mm backoff, NC wire is broken/unplugged or stuck!
    if (isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_X_HOME) || isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Y_HOME)) {
      triggerHardFault(F("X/Y NC home switch stuck open or wire broken"));
      return;
    }
  }

  // 3. Seek X & Y Normally-Closed home switches at 20 mm/s with 30 s worst-case timeout
  unsigned long homeStartMs = millis();
  while (!isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_X_HOME) || !isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Y_HOME)) {
    if (isEstopActive()) {
      triggerHardFault(F("E-Stop triggered during homing seek"));
      return;
    }
#if Z_LIMIT_IS_OVERTRAVEL
    if (isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Z_HOME)) {
      triggerHardFault(F("Z overtravel limit tripped during homing seek"));
      return;
    }
#endif
    if (millis() - homeStartMs > HOMING_TIMEOUT_MS) {
      triggerHardFault(F("Homing seek timeout (>30s): stall, belt slip, or switch failure"));
      return;
    }
    if (!isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_X_HOME)) {
      stepperX.setSpeed(-HOMING_SPEED_STEPS);
      stepperX.runSpeed();
    }
    if (!isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Y_HOME)) {
      stepperY.setSpeed(-HOMING_SPEED_STEPS);
      stepperY.runSpeed();
    }
    serviceSerialDuringHoming();
  }

  // 4. Back off 3.0 mm off switches and VERIFY switches re-close (LOW) within timeout
  stepperX.setCurrentPosition(0);
  stepperY.setCurrentPosition(0);
  stepperX.moveTo(lroundf(3.0f * STEPS_PER_MM_X));
  stepperY.moveTo(lroundf(3.0f * STEPS_PER_MM_Y));
  unsigned long releaseStartMs = millis();
  while (!isGantryAtTarget()) {
    if (isEstopActive()) {
      triggerHardFault(F("E-Stop triggered during homing switch release"));
      return;
    }
    if (millis() - releaseStartMs > BACKOFF_TIMEOUT_MS) {
      triggerHardFault(F("Homing switch release backoff timeout"));
      return;
    }
    stepperX.run();
    stepperY.run();
    serviceSerialDuringHoming();
  }
  if (isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_X_HOME) || isAxisLimitOpen(PIN_Y_HOME)) {
    triggerHardFault(F("X/Y home switch did not re-close after 3mm backoff"));
    return;
  }

  stepperX.setCurrentPosition(0);
  stepperY.setCurrentPosition(0);

  // 5. Restore operating speed (2800 steps/s = 70 mm/s) & acceleration (6000 steps/s^2 = 150 mm/s^2)
  stepperX.setMaxSpeed(MAX_SPEED_STEPS);
  stepperX.setAcceleration(ACCEL_STEPS);
  stepperY.setMaxSpeed(MAX_SPEED_STEPS);
  stepperY.setAcceleration(ACCEL_STEPS);

  // Drain any stale bytes before declaring READY
  while (Serial1.available() > 0) {
    Serial1.read();
  }

  isHomed         = true;
  lastHeartbeatMs = millis();
  currentState    = STATE_IDLE;
  Serial1.write(CMD_STATUS_READY);
  Serial.println(F("[READY] Homing complete. Gantry at (0, 0)."));
}

bool readSerial1Bytes(uint8_t *dest, uint16_t len, unsigned long timeoutMs) {
  // Called only during STATE_IDLE when steppers are stationary; drains 64B FIFO at max rate
  unsigned long start = millis();
  uint16_t idx = 0;
  while (idx < len) {
    if (isEstopActive()) {
      triggerHardFault(F("E-Stop triggered during batch receive"));
      return false;
    }
    if (Serial1.available() > 0) {
      dest[idx++] = (uint8_t)Serial1.read();
    } else if (millis() - start > timeoutMs) {
      return false;
    }
  }
  return true;
}

void pollVisionHostPackets() {
  while (Serial1.available() > 0) {
    uint8_t peekByte = (uint8_t)Serial1.read();

    // 1. Explicit Host Readiness Query
    if (peekByte == CMD_QUERY_STATUS) {
      replyStatusQuery();
      continue;
    }

    // 2. Heartbeat Keep-Alive Ping (never auto-clears faults or initiates motion!)
    if (peekByte == CMD_HEARTBEAT_PING) {
      lastHeartbeatMs = millis();
      continue;
    }

    // 3. Explicit Operator Re-Arm & Re-Home Command from Fault/Halt State
    if (peekByte == CMD_REARM_HOME) {
      if (currentState == STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT || currentState == STATE_HARD_FAULT) {
        if (isEstopActive()) {
          Serial.println(F("[REARM_REJECTED] Release physical E-Stop before re-arming."));
          Serial1.write(CMD_STATUS_FAULT);
          continue;
        }
        Serial.println(F("[REARM] Explicit re-arm accepted; starting full re-home."));
        homeAxes();
      } else {
        replyStatusQuery();
      }
      continue;
    }

    // 4. Closed-Loop Clear-FOV Park Command (move gantry to (0,0) for unobstructed verification scan)
    if (peekByte == CMD_PARK_FOR_SCAN) {
      if ((currentState != STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET && currentState != STATE_IDLE) ||
          !isHomed || commsFaultLatched || isEstopActive()) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        continue;
      }
      if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
        triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis not retracted before clear-FOV park"));
        return;
      }
      closedLoopMode  = true;
      lastHeartbeatMs = millis();
      moveGantryToMm(0.0f, 0.0f);
      currentState    = STATE_PARKING_FOR_SCAN;
      continue;
    }

    // 5. Closed-Loop Session Finish Command (park at (0,0) if needed, emit COMPLETE -> IDLE)
    if (peekByte == CMD_FINISH_SESSION) {
      if ((currentState != STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET && currentState != STATE_IDLE) ||
          !isHomed || commsFaultLatched || isEstopActive()) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        continue;
      }
      if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
        triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis not retracted before session finish park"));
        return;
      }
      closedLoopMode  = false;
      lastHeartbeatMs = millis();
      moveGantryToMm(0.0f, 0.0f);
      currentState    = STATE_PARKING_HOME;
      continue;
    }

    // 6. Closed-Loop Single-Target Pick Packet: [0x5B][CapTarget 15B][CRC16 2B] (18B total)
    if (peekByte == PROTOCOL_SINGLE_MAGIC) {
      bool canAccept = (currentState == STATE_IDLE || currentState == STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET);
      if (!canAccept || !isHomed || commsFaultLatched || isEstopActive()) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        continue;
      }

      CapTarget rxCap;
      uint8_t crcBuf[2];
      if (!readSerial1Bytes((uint8_t*)&rxCap, sizeof(CapTarget), 250) ||
          !readSerial1Bytes(crcBuf, 2, 100)) {
        if (currentState == STATE_IDLE || currentState == STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET) {
          Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        }
        return;
      }

      uint16_t rxCrc   = (uint16_t)crcBuf[0] | ((uint16_t)crcBuf[1] << 8);
      uint16_t calcCrc = computeCRC16((const uint8_t*)&rxCap, sizeof(CapTarget));
      if (calcCrc != rxCrc || !isValidSingleTarget(rxCap)) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        return;
      }

      if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
        triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis not retracted before single-target start"));
        return;
      }

      // Valid closed-loop target! Move directly from current pose (0,0 or bin) to cap
      closedLoopMode    = true;
      batchTotal        = 1;
      currentIdx        = 0;
      currentCap        = rxCap;
      hasInFlightCap    = false;
      commsFaultLatched = false;
      lastHeartbeatMs   = millis();
      Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_ACK);

      moveGantryToMm(currentCap.boardX_mm, currentCap.boardY_mm);
      currentState = STATE_MOVING_TO_CAP;
      return;
    }

    // 7. Legacy Multi-Cap Batch Header (ONLY accepted when homed, fault-free, and IDLE)
    if (peekByte == PROTOCOL_MAGIC_BYTE) {
      if (currentState != STATE_IDLE || !isHomed || commsFaultLatched || isEstopActive()) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        continue;
      }

      BatchHeader hdr;
      hdr.magic = PROTOCOL_MAGIC_BYTE;
      if (!readSerial1Bytes(((uint8_t*)&hdr) + 1, sizeof(BatchHeader) - 1, 250)) {
        if (currentState == STATE_IDLE) Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        return;
      }
      if (hdr.totalCaps == 0 || hdr.totalCaps > MAX_BATCH_CAPS) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        return;
      }

      uint16_t payloadBytes = (uint16_t)hdr.totalCaps * sizeof(CapTarget);
      if (!readSerial1Bytes((uint8_t*)batchQueue, payloadBytes, 1500)) {
        if (currentState == STATE_IDLE) Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        return;
      }

      uint16_t calcCrc = computeCRC16((const uint8_t*)batchQueue, payloadBytes);
      if (calcCrc != hdr.crc16) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
        return;
      }
      for (uint8_t i = 0; i < hdr.totalCaps; i++) {
        if (!isValidSingleTarget(batchQueue[i])) {
          Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_NACK);
          return;
        }
      }

      if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
        triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis not retracted before batch start"));
        return;
      }

      // Valid legacy batch received! Store locally & acknowledge
      closedLoopMode    = false;
      batchTotal        = hdr.totalCaps;
      currentIdx        = 0;
      hasInFlightCap    = false;
      commsFaultLatched = false;
      lastHeartbeatMs   = millis();
      Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_ACK);

      // Start Step 01 on first target cap
      currentCap = batchQueue[0];
      moveGantryToMm(currentCap.boardX_mm, currentCap.boardY_mm);
      currentState = STATE_MOVING_TO_CAP;
    }
  }
}

void checkRuntimeSafetyInputs() {
  if (currentState == STATE_HOMING || currentState == STATE_HARD_FAULT) return;

  // Priority 1: Auxiliary NC E-Stop contact (2 consecutive samples)
  if (isEstopActive()) {
    estopFaultCount++;
    if (estopFaultCount >= 2) {
      triggerHardFault(F("Physical E-Stop auxiliary contact open"));
      return;
    }
  } else {
    estopFaultCount = 0;
  }

  // Priority 2: Polled NC limit switches for X, Y, and Z (3-sample debounce)
  if (isAnyOvertravelLimitOpen()) {
    limitFaultCount++;
    if (limitFaultCount >= 3) {
      triggerHardFault(F("Runtime X/Y/Z NC limit switch open or broken wire"));
    }
  } else {
    limitFaultCount = 0;
  }
}

bool checkCommunicationLoss() {
  if (currentState == STATE_IDLE || currentState == STATE_HOMING ||
      currentState == STATE_FAILSAFE_PARKING || currentState == STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT ||
      currentState == STATE_HARD_FAULT || commsFaultLatched) {
    return false;
  }
  return (millis() - lastHeartbeatMs > COMMS_TIMEOUT_MS);
}

void executeSafeCommsLossPolicy() {
  commsFaultLatched = true;
  Serial1.write(CMD_RECOVERY_HALT);

  // If a single scanned cap has already been dispatched and is currently in its
  // pick-and-place cycle, finish depositing ONLY that one cap into its bin,
  // then park cleanly at (0, 0) via STATE_FAILSAFE_PARKING and latch halt.
  // (Hardwired E-stop Pin 25 and NC X/Y/Z limits remain active at all times.)
  bool inActiveCapCycle = (currentState == STATE_MOVING_TO_CAP ||
                           currentState == STATE_LOWER_Z ||
                           currentState == STATE_GRIP_CAP ||
                           currentState == STATE_LIFT_Z ||
                           currentState == STATE_TRAVERSE_BIN ||
                           currentState == STATE_BIN_SETTLE ||
                           currentState == STATE_LOWER_BIN ||
                           currentState == STATE_RELEASE_CAP ||
                           currentState == STATE_RETRACT_Z);
  if (inActiveCapCycle) {
    batchTotal = 0; // Do not start any further queued caps after this one
    Serial.println(F("[COMMS_LOSS] Finishing current scanned cap into bin, then parking at (0,0) to halt."));
    return;
  }

  // No cap currently active (e.g., awaiting next target at bin or parking):
  // Confirm Z is safely retracted, park cleanly at (0, 0), disable steppers,
  // and latch STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT until explicit CMD_REARM_HOME + re-home.
  pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_RAISED);
  pca.setPWM(SERVO_GRIPPER_CH, 0, PWM_GRIP_OPEN);
  if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
    triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis not retracted on comms-loss park"));
    return;
  }
  closedLoopMode = false;
  hasInFlightCap = false;
  batchTotal     = 0;
  currentIdx     = 0;
  moveGantryToMm(0.0f, 0.0f);
  currentState   = STATE_FAILSAFE_PARKING;
  Serial.println(F("[COMMS_LOSS] No active cap; parking at (0,0) then latching fail-safe halt."));
}

void executeMotionStateMachine() {
  switch (currentState) {
    case STATE_IDLE:
    case STATE_HOMING:
    case STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET:
    case STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT:
    case STATE_HARD_FAULT:
      break;

    // Step 01: Traverse to Cap (X, Y) with Z raised and Gripper open
    case STATE_MOVING_TO_CAP:
      if (isGantryAtTarget()) {
        pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_LOWER_PICK);
        stateTimerMs = millis();
        currentState = STATE_LOWER_Z;
      }
      break;

    // Step 02: Lower Z linkage (~40 mm) over cap
    case STATE_LOWER_Z:
      if (millis() - stateTimerMs >= 350) {
        pca.setPWM(SERVO_GRIPPER_CH, 0, PWM_GRIP_CLOSE);
        stateTimerMs = millis();
        currentState = STATE_GRIP_CAP;
      }
      break;

    // Step 03: Clamp Gripper jaws (~26 mm)
    case STATE_GRIP_CAP:
      if (millis() - stateTimerMs >= 300) {
        hasInFlightCap = true; // Commanded grip complete (unverified unless HAS_GRIP_SENSOR == 1)
        pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_RAISED);
        stateTimerMs = millis();
        currentState = STATE_LIFT_Z;
      }
      break;

    // Step 04: Lift Z linkage back to 0 mm overhead clearance
    case STATE_LIFT_Z:
      if (millis() - stateTimerMs >= 350) {
        if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
          triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis failed retracted check before bin traverse"));
          return;
        }
        uint8_t binId = constrain(currentCap.targetBinId, 1, 4);
        moveGantryToMm(BIN_X_MM[binId], BIN_Y_MM[binId]);
        currentState = STATE_TRAVERSE_BIN;
      }
      break;

    // Step 05: Traverse to designated color bin; pause briefly to settle before bin-side capture
    case STATE_TRAVERSE_BIN:
      if (isGantryAtTarget()) {
        if (closedLoopMode && !commsFaultLatched) {
          stateTimerMs = millis();
          currentState = STATE_BIN_SETTLE;
        } else {
          pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_LOWER_BIN);
          stateTimerMs = millis();
          currentState = STATE_LOWER_BIN;
        }
      }
      break;

    // Step 05b: Stationary 120 ms XY settle at bin (Z raised) -> trigger pipelined camera scan
    case STATE_BIN_SETTLE:
      if (commsFaultLatched || (millis() - stateTimerMs >= BIN_SETTLE_MS)) {
        if (!commsFaultLatched) {
          Serial1.write(CMD_SCAN_WINDOW_OPEN);
        }
        pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_LOWER_BIN);
        stateTimerMs = millis();
        currentState = STATE_LOWER_BIN;
      }
      break;

    // Step 06: Lower Z partially (~25 mm) into bin opening (in parallel with vision analysis!)
    case STATE_LOWER_BIN:
      if (millis() - stateTimerMs >= 250) {
        pca.setPWM(SERVO_GRIPPER_CH, 0, PWM_GRIP_OPEN);
        stateTimerMs = millis();
        currentState = STATE_RELEASE_CAP;
      }
      break;

    // Step 07: Release Gripper jaws (cap drops into bin)
    case STATE_RELEASE_CAP:
      if (millis() - stateTimerMs >= 250) {
        hasInFlightCap = false;
        pca.setPWM(SERVO_Z_CH, 0, PWM_Z_RAISED);
        stateTimerMs = millis();
        currentState = STATE_RETRACT_Z;
      }
      break;

    // Step 08: Retract Z to 0 mm, emit progress pulse, await next closed-loop target or finish
    case STATE_RETRACT_Z:
      if (millis() - stateTimerMs >= 300) {
        if (!isZRetractedSafeForXY()) {
          triggerHardFault(F("Z-axis failed retracted check after bin release"));
          return;
        }

        Serial1.write(CMD_CAP_PROGRESS);

        if (commsFaultLatched) {
          // Active scanned cap deposit finished; park cleanly at (0,0) then latch halt
          Serial1.write(CMD_RECOVERY_HALT);
          closedLoopMode = false;
          batchTotal     = 0;
          currentIdx     = 0;
          moveGantryToMm(0.0f, 0.0f);
          currentState   = STATE_FAILSAFE_PARKING;
          return;
        }

        if (closedLoopMode) {
          batchTotal = 0;
          currentIdx = 0;
          Serial1.write(CMD_AWAIT_NEXT);
          currentState = STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET;
          return;
        }

        currentIdx++;
        if (currentIdx < batchTotal) {
          currentCap = batchQueue[currentIdx];
          moveGantryToMm(currentCap.boardX_mm, currentCap.boardY_mm);
          currentState = STATE_MOVING_TO_CAP;
        } else {
          // Legacy batch finished! Return gantry to (0,0) clear of camera FOV
          moveGantryToMm(0.0f, 0.0f);
          currentState = STATE_PARKING_HOME;
        }
      }
      break;

    // Clear-FOV verification park (gantry clears bridge off board, stays in active session)
    case STATE_PARKING_FOR_SCAN:
      if (isGantryAtTarget()) {
        Serial1.write(CMD_AWAIT_NEXT);
        currentState = STATE_AWAIT_NEXT_TARGET;
      }
      break;

    case STATE_PARKING_HOME:
      if (isGantryAtTarget()) {
        closedLoopMode = false;
        batchTotal     = 0;
        currentIdx     = 0;
        Serial1.write(CMD_BATCH_COMPLETE);
        currentState = STATE_IDLE;
      }
      break;

    case STATE_FAILSAFE_PARKING:
      if (isGantryAtTarget()) {
        digitalWrite(PIN_STEPPER_EN, HIGH);
        closedLoopMode = false;
        isHomed        = false;
        hasInFlightCap = false;
        batchTotal     = 0;
        currentIdx     = 0;
        currentState   = STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT;
        Serial1.write(CMD_RECOVERY_HALT);
        Serial.println(F("[COMMS_LOSS] Parked at (0,0) & halted. Explicit re-arm & re-home required."));
      }
      break;
  }
}

void setup() {
  // Keep actuators disabled during power-up until E-stop & I2C are initialized
  pinMode(PIN_SERVO_OE, OUTPUT);
  digitalWrite(PIN_SERVO_OE, HIGH);

  pinMode(PIN_STEPPER_EN, OUTPUT);
  digitalWrite(PIN_STEPPER_EN, HIGH);

  pinMode(PIN_X_HOME, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_Y_HOME, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_Z_HOME, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_ESTOP,  INPUT_PULLUP);

#if ENABLE_MAX_ENDSTOPS
  pinMode(PIN_X_MAX, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_Y_MAX, INPUT_PULLUP);
#endif
#if HAS_GRIP_SENSOR
  pinMode(PIN_GRIP_SENSE, INPUT_PULLUP);
#endif

  Serial.begin(115200);   // USB Debug
  Serial1.begin(115200);  // Vision Host UART (Bidirectional 5V <-> 3.3V Level Shifter)

  Serial.println(F("=== CapSorter Mega Motion Controller (Pre-Validation) ==="));
  Serial.print(F("Nominal Scale (steps/mm): X=")); Serial.print(STEPS_PER_MM_X);
  Serial.print(F(" Y=")); Serial.println(STEPS_PER_MM_Y);
  Serial.print(F("Tool Offset (mm): dX=")); Serial.print(TOOL_OFFSET_X_MM);
  Serial.print(F(" dY=")); Serial.println(TOOL_OFFSET_Y_MM);
  if (TOOL_OFFSET_X_MM == 0.0f && TOOL_OFFSET_Y_MM == 0.0f) {
    Serial.println(F("[WARN] TOOL_OFFSET is (0,0) placeholder; measure physical alignment on bench."));
  }

  Wire.begin();
  Wire.setClock(400000);  // 400 kHz Fast-Mode I2C
  pca.begin();
  pca.setPWMFreq(50);     // 50 Hz analog servo PWM

  homeAxes();
}

void loop() {
  // 1. Poll E-stop auxiliary contact and X/Y/Z NC limit switches
  checkRuntimeSafetyInputs();

  // 2. Step only when actively moving and fault-free
  if (currentState != STATE_HARD_FAULT && currentState != STATE_FAILSAFE_HALT) {
    stepperX.run();
    stepperY.run();
  }

  // 3. Poll host status queries, batch packets, heartbeat pings & re-arm commands
  pollVisionHostPackets();

  // 4. Execute non-blocking 8-step pick-and-place state machine
  executeMotionStateMachine();

  // 5. Watchdog: latch fail-safe halt if UART heartbeat drops during active motion
  if (checkCommunicationLoss()) {
    executeSafeCommsLossPolicy();
  }
}
// =========================================================================
// packet_protocol.h - Shared Inter-Controller Wire Definitions (V1 & V2)
// Ensures Type Safety, CRC16-CCITT Checking, Explicit Handshake & Supervision
// Python Packing Formats:
//   SingleTarget: struct.pack('<B', 0x5B) + CapTarget[15B] + struct.pack('<H', crc16) [18B]
//   BatchHeader : struct.pack('<BHBH', magic, batchId, totalCaps, crc16) [6B]
//   CapTarget   : struct.pack('<BBHHffB', id, color, pxX, pxY, x_mm, y_mm, bin) [15B]
// =========================================================================
#ifndef PACKET_PROTOCOL_H
#define PACKET_PROTOCOL_H

#include <stdint.h>

// Shared Batch Capacity & Continuous Board Reachability Limits
#define MAX_BATCH_CAPS        100   // Maximum CapTarget entries accepted in one legacy batch
#define BOARD_SIZE_MM         300.0f // Continuous 300x300 mm open flat plywood sorting plate
#define CAP_DIAMETER_NOM_MM    30.0f // Nominal beverage bottle cap outer diameter (28-32 mm)
#define SAFE_PICK_MIN_X_MM     18.0f // Min safe cap center X (keeps rim inside plate & clear of X-home)
#define SAFE_PICK_MAX_X_MM    282.0f // Max safe cap center X (keeps rim inside 300 mm plate)
#define SAFE_PICK_MIN_Y_MM     18.0f // Min safe cap center Y (keeps rim inside plate & clear of Y-home)
#define SAFE_PICK_MAX_Y_MM    282.0f // Max safe cap center Y (keeps rim inside 300 mm plate)

// Frame, Legacy Batch & Closed-Loop Single-Target Magic Bytes

// Shared CRC16-CCITT implementation (Polynomial 0x1021, Initial 0xFFFF)
static inline uint16_t computeCRC16(const uint8_t* data, uint32_t length) {
  uint16_t crc = 0xFFFF;
  for (uint32_t i = 0; i < length; i++) {
    crc ^= ((uint16_t)data[i] << 8);
    for (uint8_t bit = 0; bit < 8; bit++) {
      if (crc & 0x8000) {
        crc = (crc << 1) ^ 0x1021;
      } else {
        crc = (crc << 1);
      }
    }
  }
  return crc;
}

Critical V2 Engineering Considerations

Essential hardware and Linux edge-computing safeguards specific to the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W + 8MP IMX219 160° camera architecture.

1. 22-Pin Pi Zero CSI Ribbon Cable

Standard 8MP IMX219 camera modules on Robu.in include a 15-pin (1.0 mm pitch) cable for Pi 3/4. The Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W has a narrower 22-pin (0.5 mm pitch) CSI connector. Always pair the camera with a Pi Zero 15-pin to 22-pin CSI adapter ribbon cable (~₹69).

2. 160° Fisheye Lens Rectification

A 160° wide-angle lens exhibits radial barrel distortion near the perimeter of the 300×300 mm board. A 3×3 planar homography matrix only corrects flat perspective tilt—you must run cv2.fisheye.initUndistortRectifyMap + cv2.remap before applying homography.

3. Read-Only OverlayFS for 16GB SD

Linux microSD cards can suffer ext4 corruption if 12V main power is switched off without running sudo shutdown. Enable OverlayFS (Read-Only Root Filesystem) in raspi-config once calibrated so the Pi Zero 2 W can be safely powered off instantly like a microcontroller.

4. TMC2208 Vref & Pi Heatsink

Inspect the exact Rsense on your TMC2208 carrier (commonly 0.11 Ω). Per ADI TMC2208 Rev 1.13: IRMS = (0.325 / (Rsense + 0.03)) × (Vref / (2.5 × √2)) (~0.75 A RMS at Vref=1.15 V, Rsense=0.11 Ω). Measure driver & Pi heatsink thermals on the bench.

5. Dedicated Servo vs. Pi Power Rail

Never power MG90S servos from the same 5V buck converter feeding the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W. Use separate regulated branches sharing a common star ground: Branch B (5V/5A Buck) for PCA9685 V+ and Branch C (5V/3A Buck) for Pi + Mega 5V pin. Verify your exact MG90S SKU voltage rating (original TowerPro MG90S is 4.8 V nominal).

6. Locked AWB/AEC & Full-Sensor 1640×1232 Binning

In Picamera2, configuring raw={"size": (1640, 1232)} forces the 8MP IMX219 into 2×2 full-sensor binning before ISP downscaling to 640×480 (preventing center-crop FOV loss), while locking AeEnable=False, AwbEnable=False after warm-up.

7. 16 MHz AVR Step Budget (40 steps/mm)

On an 8-bit 16 MHz ATmega2560, AccelStepper::run() has a ~4,000 steps/s combined ceiling. Running TMC2208 at 1/8 microstepping (40 steps/mm nominal) yields 70 mm/s (2800 steps/s) and 0.025 mm commanded step resolution (open-loop — measure physical accuracy & repeatability on the bench).

8. 64-Byte AVR UART FIFO Pacing & Explicit Re-Arm

pi_zero_vision.py waits for CMD_STATUS_READY, streams batches in 32-byte paced chunks (2 ms pause), and services 0xF0 heartbeats across the entire batch via CMD_CAP_PROGRESS. Any comms-loss halt or E-stop requires explicit operator re-arm (CMD_REARM_HOME) & re-homing — never auto-recovering on heartbeats.

9. Specular Glare Closing, 24mm NMS & NC Wire Guard

MORPH_CLOSE seals specular highlights on glossy cap domes and 24 mm Spatial NMS prevents duplicate picks. On the Mega, Pin 9 holds PCA9685 OE HIGH during boot to eliminate servo twitch and NC switches are monitored continuously in loop().
